iPhone and iPad · iOS 18.5+

Hide photos, videos & files. Private. Encrypted. Yours.

Keepshire is a private vault with no account and no cloud. A passcode or password unlocks it, Face ID is optional, and files stay encrypted on your device.

See what’s inside Privacy policy

Everything stays on your device

A vault that works like the rest of iOS, with nothing sent anywhere.

On this phone only

No iCloud of vault files, no account, no analytics. Import from Photos or Files, or send from a browser on the same Wi‑Fi.

Locked when you leave

Choose a 4-digit or 6-digit passcode, or a password. Optional Face ID / Touch ID. Auto-lock when the app goes to the background.

Folders, gallery, preview

Nested folders, search, favorites, and previews for photos, video, audio, PDF, and other documents.

Same-Wi‑Fi import

Optional HTTPS server on your local network. Pair with a 6-digit code. Downloads need Face ID or your vault credential.

Extra cover

Screenshot blanking, App Switcher cover, optional recording overlay, shake or flip to lock, and a decoy credential that opens an empty screen.

iPhone and iPad

A full-width gallery and full-screen viewer with pinch, double-tap, and swipe on both, plus sidebars for folders and categories on iPad.

Honest limits

A short PIN can still be guessed. The decoy does not hide files from a backup or a filesystem dump. Screenshots of the vault are blank; they are not impossible. If you forget your passcode or password, your files cannot be recovered.

Questions before you start?

Unlocking, Web Upload certificates, and the decoy credential are covered in the support guide.
