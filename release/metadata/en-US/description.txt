Keepshire is a private vault for the photos, videos, audio and documents you don't want sitting in your camera roll. There is no account to create and no cloud to trust: everything stays on your iPhone or iPad, encrypted.

LOCKED THE WAY YOU WANT
• Choose a 4-digit passcode, 6-digit passcode, or a full password
• Optional Face ID or Touch ID
• Auto-lock when you leave the app: immediately, after a few seconds or minutes, or never
• The App Switcher shows a cover instead of your files

ENCRYPTED ON YOUR DEVICE
• Every file and thumbnail is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your passcode or password
• File names, types and sizes are encrypted too
• Vault files are excluded from iCloud and computer backups
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking

ORGANIZE EVERYTHING
• Nested folders you can create, rename, move and delete
• Gallery and category views: Favorites, Photos, Videos, Audio, Documents and more
• Search, sort, favorites and multi-select
• Optional trash with restore

IMPORT YOUR WAY
• Pick photos and videos from your library
• Add any file from the Files app
• Send files from the Share sheet in other apps
• Web Upload: send files from a computer's browser on the same Wi-Fi, over HTTPS, paired with a one-time code

VIEW WITHOUT LEAVING THE VAULT
• Zoom and swipe through photos
• Video player with scrubbing, skip and playback speed
• Audio playback, PDF viewer and previews for common documents

EXTRA PROTECTION
• Screenshot blanking: screenshots of the vault come out black
• Screen recording cover
• Shake or flip your phone to lock instantly
• Decoy passcode that opens an empty vault

Important: there is no back door. If you forget your vault passcode or password, your files cannot be recovered — not by us, not by anyone.
